# FlowPass: applicant guide

This page is for **people applying for the AI-tool subsidy**, not for engineers. 繁體中文版：[申請人說明.md](./申請人說明.md).

---

## In one sentence

**竹流 FlowPass** lets you describe how you use AI in LINE, confirm a data-flow record, upload purchase and identity documents, then follow review progress. Eligibility, the subsidy amount, and when money arrives are decided by Hsinchu City Government. FlowPass is the online apply-and-track helper.

---

## Who this is for

Youth in Hsinchu City who have bought (or will buy) an **approved AI tool** and are ready to apply for subsidy.

- Special-status applicants and cultural-language preservation applicants use the **same path** as everyone else. The subsidy rate may differ. Eligibility follows the city’s announcement.
- If you have not bought a tool yet, or you are unsure whether you qualify, read the official announcement first. Do not rely on LINE alone.

The official program name is **AI領航青年數位工具補助**. Age, residency, deadlines, and which tools count are defined on the Youth Development Center page:

<https://youthhsinchu.hccg.gov.tw/youth/app/artwebsite?id=64&module=artwebsite&serno=null>

## What FlowPass does — and does not do

**It helps you**

- Answer four questions about how you actually use AI
- Produce a readable **AI data passport** (data flow)
- Collect purchase details and required files, then submit
- Check progress, upload supplements, correct a case, and see tool-related security notices in LINE

**It does not**

- Let AI **decide** whether you get a subsidy, or **decide** the amount
- Confirm **exactly when** money will hit your account (that is the city’s disbursement process)
- Replace the official announcement for full eligibility rules (household registration, age, special-status definitions, and so on)

Expect about **10–15 minutes**. Open the app from the 竹流 FlowPass LINE official account menu. Do not start from a random browser search.

---

## What “passport” means

This is **not** a travel passport.

It is the **data-use description** for this application: what kind of data you handle, which AI tool you use, where it is stored, and who can see it. Reviewers use it to understand whether the process is clear and whether sensitive data is involved.

Describe **data types and purposes** only (for example: club photos, editing, stored on a club cloud). **Do not paste** real photos, personal data, passwords, or file contents.

After you submit, the system can also match tools named on the passport against **public** security incidents. A public incident is not the same as “this already applies to your case.” Personalized alerts appear only after staff confirm them.

## Three steps to apply

### Step 1: Describe your use

Answer in order:

1. What data will you handle? (types only)
2. What do you want the AI to do?
3. What personal or sensitive data might be included? (you may write 「不確定」)
4. Where will the result live, and who will see it?

Answers save as you go. Write the real process. Do not paste system prompts or ask the AI to play another role.

### Step 2: Confirm the data flow

The system drafts a data-flow summary. If something is unclear, it asks a few follow-up questions (for example the tool name). Confirm before you continue.

If the AI cannot produce a draft, the screen will ask you to rewrite your answers. It will not pretend the draft succeeded.

### Step 3: Purchase details and files

Enter the software name, vendor, purchase date, subscription period, cost, applicant name, and similar fields. The last four digits of the card and the cardholder name are used for encrypted matching. **Plain card numbers are not stored.**

Then upload the required documents and submit. After that, use **進度查詢**.

## Documents to prepare

| Document | Why |
|---|---|
| National ID, both sides | Identity and residency |
| Official receipt and card transaction | Tool, amount, and date |
| Passbook cover | Bank account for transfer |
| Affidavit | Required at submit |
| Representative-payment affidavit | **Only if someone else paid** |
| Eligibility proof | Only if you selected a special status |

Use JPG, PNG, or PDF. About 12 MB per file. Photos should be readable. Do not upload password-protected PDFs.

## After you submit

Use **進度查詢**. Common states:

- **Submitted / in review**: wait; you usually do not need to act yet
- **Needs extra files**: upload what staff asked for, then send the supplement
- **Needs correction**: change the application as instructed, then resubmit
- **Approved / awaiting payment / paid / closed**
- **Not approved**: review has ended

The amount is estimated from published rules. **Staff review is final.** Rates and caps for general youth and special-status applicants follow the city announcement and this program’s rules.

After approval and scheduling for payment, funds go to the passbook account you uploaded, and LINE notifies you of the status. **The exact deposit date cannot be confirmed here.**

---

## Amount and eligibility (city announcement wins)

Inside the product, applicants currently see this estimate:

- General youth: 50% of the eligible purchase, cap NT$3,000
- Special status (for example low-income / middle-low-income households) and cultural-language preservation: 90% of the eligible purchase, cap NT$6,000

Who may apply, age, residency, purchase window, and which tools qualify **always follow the city announcement**. The reviewer’s decision is final.

---

## How personal data is handled

Data needed for review and to prevent duplicate claims is stored encrypted. Card last-four and cardholder name are used only as an encrypted fingerprint. The system does not keep a plaintext card number.
